How to Play Australian Patience

The Goal: Build all 4 Foundation piles (top right) UP by exact suit from Ace to King.

Tableau Rules (The Twist!)

  • The Tableau (main board) is built DOWN by exact suit. (e.g., place a 9♥ on a 10♥).
  • The Golden Rule: You can pick up and move ANY face-up card, regardless of what cards are stacked on top of it! The entire stack moves as a single unit.
  • By default, empty tableau columns can ONLY be filled with a King.

Stock & Waste

  • Tap the Stock pile to deal 1 card to the Waste pile.
  • By default, you get No Redeals. You only pass through the deck once!

Game Variants (Settings ⚙️)

🐨 Brisbane Variant

Changes the empty space rule so that any card can fill an empty column (not just Kings).

🦘 Canberra Variant

In addition to allowing any card in empty spaces, the Tableau is built down by Alternate Colors instead of exact suits!
