How to Play

The Goal: Remove all 52 cards from the deck by matching pairs of identical ranks (e.g., two Queens, two 8s).

Rules of Matching

  • You can only remove pairs of cards that are adjacent to each other (touching horizontally, vertically, or diagonally).
  • Simply tap one card, then tap a matching card to remove them!
  • There is no dragging in Monte Carlo.

Consolidation

  • When you run out of matches (or whenever you want to), press the "Consolidate & Deal" button.
  • All cards on the board will slide up and to the left to fill the empty gaps, retaining their exact order.
  • New cards will be dealt from the stock to refill the 25-card grid.

⚙️ Variants Available in Settings!

Monte Carlo features 4 classic rule variants ranging from the strict "Orthogonal Only" to the wildly casual "Match Anywhere". Check the settings gear!
