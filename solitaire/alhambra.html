How to Play Alhambra

The Goal: Alhambra uses TWO full decks (104 cards). Move all cards to the 8 Foundation piles at the top.

Foundation Rules

  • Aces (Left 4): Build UP by exact suit to King.
  • Kings (Right 4): Build DOWN by exact suit to Ace.
  • One Ace and one King of each suit are dealt to the foundations automatically at the start of the game!

Tableau & Waste Rules

  • Reserves (Bottom 8 columns): You cannot build cards on the reserves! Empty slots cannot be reused.
  • The Waste Pile (Center): This is the key to Alhambra. You can temporarily build cards from the reserves onto the Waste pile to access the cards underneath them!
  • You build on the Waste pile UP OR DOWN by suit (wrapping King to Ace).

⚙️ Variants Available in Settings!

Alhambra is notoriously difficult to win. We highly recommend using the Relaxed Waste Building variant, which lets you build on the waste pile with ANY suit, rather than matching the exact suit!
