WENDY'S CALCULATION

MOVES: 0

How to Play Calculation

The Goal: Calculation ignores suits completely! Your goal is to build 4 foundation piles based entirely on mathematical intervals.

The Math Foundations

  • Pile 1 (Starts with Ace): Counts by 1s (A, 2, 3, 4, 5...)
  • Pile 2 (Starts with 2): Counts by 2s (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, Q, A, 3...)
  • Pile 3 (Starts with 3): Counts by 3s (3, 6, 9, Q, 2, 5, 8...)
  • Pile 4 (Starts with 4): Counts by 4s (4, 8, Q, 3, 7, J...)
  • Wendy's Tip: We render faint "Ghost Hints" on the foundations so you never have to do the math in your head!

The Play Area

  • The Stock automatically deals 1 card into the "Dealt" slot.
  • You MUST play this Dealt card to a foundation or to one of the 4 Tableau columns below before another card is dealt!
  • Tableau Rules: You can drop any card into any of the 4 Tableau columns. However, you can never move a card from one Tableau to another! They can only move to a Foundation.

⚙️ Variants Available in Settings!

If you prefer a more forgiving game, turn on "Relaxed" Tableau rules to allow moving top cards between the tableaus. Hardcore players can also disable the math Ghost Hints!
