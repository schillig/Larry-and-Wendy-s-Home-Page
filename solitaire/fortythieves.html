WENDY'S FORTY THIEVES

MOVES: 0

How to Play

The Goal: Forty Thieves uses TWO full decks (104 cards). Move all cards to the 8 Foundation piles (top right). Foundations are built UP by exact suit from Ace to King.

General Rules

  • The Tableau (main board) is built DOWN by exact suit. (e.g., place a 9♥ on a 10♥).
  • Empty tableau columns can be filled with any card.
  • Tap a card without dragging to automatically fly it to a foundation if it fits!

Game Modes (Settings ⚙️)

👑 Wendy's Rules (Default)

A fun, modern way to play! You can move properly ordered sequences as a group. If the Stock pile empties, tap it to redeal the Waste pile infinitely.

⚖️ Original Rules (Brutal)

The true "Napoleon at St. Helena" challenge. You may ONLY move one card at a time. You can only flip through the Stock pile ONCE (no redeals). Less than 5% of games are winnable!
