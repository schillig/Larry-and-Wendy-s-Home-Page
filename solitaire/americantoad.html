How to Play American Toad

The Goal: American Toad is a challenging two-deck variant of Canfield. Move all 104 cards to the 8 Foundation piles to win!

Foundations & Base Rank

  • The very first card dealt establishes the Base Rank (shown in the top center).
  • All 8 foundations must start with this Base Rank, and build UP by exact suit, wrapping from King back to Ace.

Tableau & Reserve Rules

  • Build the 8 Tableau columns DOWN by exact suit, wrapping from Ace to King.
  • Moving: You can only move the single top card of a column OR the entire column. You cannot move a partial column!
  • Empty Spaces: Empty columns are automatically filled by the top card of the 20-card Reserve pile.
  • If the Reserve is empty, an empty space can only be filled by a card from the Waste or Reserve, not from another tableau column.

Stock Rules

  • Turn over 1 card at a time. You get exactly 1 redeal (two total passes through the deck).

⚙️ Variants Available in Settings!

If the original rules are too strict, use the Settings menu to toggle "Relaxed" modes, allowing you to build by alternating colors, move partial sequences, or fill empty columns with any card!
