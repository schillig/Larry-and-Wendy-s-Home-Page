WENDY'S SIR TOMMY

MOVES: 0

How to Play Sir Tommy

The Goal: Build all 4 foundation piles from Ace to King. Unlike Klondike, you can build the foundations regardless of suit!

Dealing & The Tableau

  • The Stock automatically deals exactly 1 card into the "Dealt" slot.
  • You MUST play this card to a foundation or to one of the 4 Tableau columns below before another card is dealt!
  • Tableaus: You can drop any card onto any of the 4 Tableau columns. There are no sequence rules for tableaus!
  • The Catch: By default, once a card is placed on a tableau, it can never be moved to another tableau. It can only move to a Foundation. Plan carefully! Keep Kings at the bottom of your piles.

⚙️ Variants Available in Settings!

Turn on Relaxed Tableau Movement to allow shifting top cards between tableau piles. Or, try the famous "Strategy" Variant which forces you to build Foundations by exact matching suit!
