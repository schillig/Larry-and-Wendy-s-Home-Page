How to Play Pyramid

The Goal: Remove all 28 cards from the pyramid by finding pairs that add up exactly to 13.

Card Values & Matching

  • Numbered cards equal their face value (e.g., 6 + 7 = 13).
  • Aces = 1, Jacks = 11, Queens = 12.
  • Kings = 13. Since they equal 13 by themselves, you can simply tap a King to remove it instantly!
  • You can match any two exposed cards in the pyramid, or match an exposed pyramid card with the top card of the Waste pile.

Rules of the Pyramid

  • A card in the pyramid is only "exposed" and playable if it has no cards overlapping it from the row below. Buried cards will appear darkened until freed.
  • Tap the Stock (top left) to deal new cards to the Waste pile.
  • Wendy's Dual Controls: You can either tap one card, then tap a second to match them—OR you can literally drag one card and drop it onto its partner!

⚙️ Variants Available in Settings!

Customize your game by choosing to draw 1 card or 3 cards at a time. You can also restrict the number of Redeals to increase the challenge.
