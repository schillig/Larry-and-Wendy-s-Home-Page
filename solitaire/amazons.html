How to Play Amazons

The Goal: Amazons is a fast-paced game played with a stripped 28-card deck (Aces, 7s, 8s, 9s, 10s, Jacks, and Queens only). Build all 4 foundation piles up in sequence to the Queens to win!

Foundations & Sequences

  • Foundations must start with an Ace.
  • Build upwards in exact suit: A, 7, 8, 9, 10, J, Q.
  • There is no building allowed on the tableau. You can only move the top card of a tableau column directly to the foundations.

Redealing (The Secret Strategy)

  • When you are stuck, click the Stock or Redeal button.
  • This gathers all the cards on the board from left to right, flips them face down, and deals 4 new cards.
  • This shifting mechanism changes the order of the cards, slowly unblocking buried sequences! Redeals are unlimited.

⚙️ Variants Available in Settings!

In the Strict (Original) rules, a card can only be played to the foundation directly above its column (except Queens, which fly anywhere). We highly recommend playing on the default Relaxed mode, which allows any card to play to any valid foundation!
