WENDY'S GAPS

SCORE: 0
MOVES: 0

How to Play

The Goal: Arrange all 4 rows into perfect sequences from 2 to King of the same suit. The gaps should end up at the far right of each row.

Filling Gaps

  • A gap can be filled by the card that is exactly one rank higher and of the same suit as the card to its left.
  • If a gap is at the far left edge of a row, it can be filled by any 2.
  • If a gap is to the right of a King, it is a "dead gap" and nothing can fill it!

Wendy's Controls & Redeals

  • Smart Tap: Just tap a card! If there is a valid gap for it, it will fly there automatically!
  • Smart Hints: Look closely at empty gaps! They display a faint hint indicating exactly which card belongs there.
  • Redeals: When you are stuck, press REDEAL. All cards in a perfect sequence (starting from the left edge) will be locked in green. Everything else gets picked up, shuffled, and redealt, giving you new gaps to work with!
