How to Play

The Goal: Discard all 48 cards into the discard pile until ONLY the 4 Aces remain on the board!

Rules of Discarding

  • Look at the top exposed card of all 4 columns.
  • If two top cards are the SAME SUIT, you can tap the LOWER card to instantly discard it!
  • (Remember: Aces are the HIGHEST card in this game, so you never want to discard them!)

Dealing & Empty Spaces

  • When you run out of discards, tap the Stock Pile (top left) to deal 4 new cards across the board.
  • If you clear an entire column, you can drag the top card of any other pile into the empty space to free up cards underneath it!

⚙️ Variants Available in Settings!

Change what cards are allowed to move into Empty Spaces. Play the classic "Any Card" rules, or test your skills with "Kings Only" or the brutal "No Moving" rules!
