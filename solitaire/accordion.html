WENDY'S ACCORDION

PILES LEFT: 52

How to Play Accordion

The Goal: Compress all 52 cards into a single pile! This game is notoriously difficult to beat perfectly. Finishing with 5 or fewer piles is considered a major victory.

Rules of Movement

  • You may drag and drop a pile onto the pile exactly 1 space to its left OR exactly 3 spaces to its left.
  • To make a move, the top card of both piles must match in either SUIT (e.g. ♠ on ♠) or RANK (e.g. 7 on 7).
  • When you move a pile, the entire stack underneath goes with it.
  • Any gaps left behind automatically close up (like an accordion!), shifting all cards left.

Wendy's Controls

  • Smart Tap: Just tap a card! If it only has 1 legal move, it flies there automatically. If it has 2 choices, it highlights in gold so you can tap your destination!
  • Pro Tip: Always prefer jumping 3 spaces over 1 space when given the choice!
