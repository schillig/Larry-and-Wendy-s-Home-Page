WENDY'S ACME

REDEALS: 1
MOVES: 0

How to Play

The Goal: Move all 52 cards onto the 4 foundation piles, building each UP in suit from Ace to King. (The 4 Aces are automatically dealt to the foundations at the start!).

Game Rules

  • Tableau (Bottom 4 columns): Build cards DOWN in suit (e.g., place a 9♥ on a 10♥).
  • Reserve (Middle Right): You have a 13-card reserve pile. If a Tableau column ever becomes empty, the top Reserve card will automatically fly in to fill it!
  • Once the Reserve is completely empty, you can move any valid card into an empty Tableau slot.
  • Stock & Waste: You only get ONE REDEAL! (Two passes through the deck total). Use it wisely!

Variants (Settings ⚙️)

👑 Acme (Strict 1-Card)

The brutally difficult original rules. You can only ever move one card at a time. Sequences cannot be dragged!

⚙️ Acme II (Move Sequences)

A more forgiving variant. You are allowed to drag and move fully packed sequences (e.g., dragging 10♥-9♥-8♥ together).
