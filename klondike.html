How to Play Klondike

The Goal: Build all four suits up from Ace to King in the top right Foundations.

General Rules

  • Tableau (Bottom): Build piles down by alternating colors (e.g., placing a Red 6 on a Black 7).
  • Foundations (Top Right): Build piles up starting with an Ace, matching the exact suit.
  • Moving Stacks: You can move a face-up stack of cards if they are in descending order and alternating colors.
  • Empty Columns: Only a King (or a stack starting with a King) can be placed in an empty Tableau column.
  • Stock (Top Left): Tap to reveal cards. You can choose to draw 1 or 3 cards at a time in the settings!
