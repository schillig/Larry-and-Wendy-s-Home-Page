How to Play FreeCell

The Goal: Build all four suits up from Ace to King in the top right Foundations.

General Rules

  • Tableau (Bottom): Build columns down by alternating colors (e.g., place a Red 6 on a Black 7).
  • Free Cells (Top Left): Four storage slots. Each holds one temporary card.
  • Foundations (Top Right): Build piles up starting with an Ace, matching the exact suit.
  • Empty Columns: Any card or valid stack can be placed in an empty Tableau column.

🧠 Pro-Tip: Moving Stacks

Unlike other solitaires, you can only move a stack of cards if you have enough empty Free Cells or empty Tableau columns to technically move them one by one. Our engine calculates this math for you instantly!
